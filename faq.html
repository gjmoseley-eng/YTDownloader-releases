YTDownloader — FAQ

Video & audio downloads, no sketchy sites required
macOS says it can't verify the app, or asks for network permission — is that normal?

Yes. Since YTDownloader isn't distributed through the Mac App Store, macOS may show a warning the first time you open it — click Open to continue. You may also be asked for permission to access your local network, which the app needs to check for updates and, on the church's shared computer, to talk to other tools. Both are normal and safe to allow.

Why is it asking me to sign in to YouTube?

YouTube occasionally requires sign-in to confirm you're not a bot before allowing certain downloads. A sign-in window will open inside the app — log in there, click Done, and try again.

Why does the file download as .mp4 (or .m4a for audio)?

YTDownloader always converts downloads into these formats since they play reliably everywhere, even when YouTube's original file was in a different format behind the scenes.

Why can't I find a 4K option for some videos?

Not every video has a 4K version, and very high resolutions and frame rates aren't always available. Whatever appears in the list is what YouTube is offering for that specific video.

Where do my downloads go?

Wherever your "Save to" folder is set to — the Downloads folder by default. You can change it anytime.

Do I need to be connected to the internet?

Yes, downloads and format fetching both require an active internet connection.

What's the difference between "Check for Updates" and "Check for yt-dlp Update"?

"Check for Updates" (in the app menu) updates YTDownloader itself. "Check for yt-dlp Update" updates the internal engine that actually talks to YouTube — this matters because YouTube changes things fairly often, and yt-dlp updates keep up with those changes independently of app releases.

A download failed — what do I do?

Check the Log panel at the bottom of the window for details. If it's not clear what went wrong, use Help → Send Feedback, which will include the recent log automatically.
