Frequently asked questions

Common questions about downloading with YTDownloader.

The basics

What is YTDownloader for?

Saving YouTube videos, or just their sound, as files on your Mac, so you can play them in ProPresenter, Keynote or an editor without relying on the internet or YouTube's ads. See the manual.

Is it OK to download any video?

Only download videos you have the right to use: your church's own videos, videos whose owner has said yes, or ones under a license that allows reuse. YouTube shows "Creative Commons" in a video's description when it is. Licensed music and films usually aren't covered, even for church use, unless your church has a license that includes them. When in doubt, ask before using it.

Which Macs does it run on?

Any Mac with macOS 15 or later, Intel or Apple silicon.

Does it cost anything, or need an account?

No. There's nothing to buy and no account to make. Now and then YouTube asks for a Google sign-in; see Signing in.

Does it need the internet?

Yes, to look up and download videos, and to check for updates. Files you've already downloaded play anywhere, with no internet.

Does it work with sites other than YouTube?

No, only YouTube (including youtu.be links, YouTube Music links and Shorts).

Installing and opening

macOS asks if I'm sure I want to open it. Is that normal?

Yes. YTDownloader doesn't come from the App Store, so the first time you open it macOS checks with Apple and asks you to confirm. Click Open. It won't ask again.

It asked to send notifications. Do I need them?

No, but they're handy: you'll be told when a download finishes while you're in another app, with a button that shows the file in Finder. To change it later, go to System Settings > Notifications > YTDownloader.

Where should the app live?

In your Applications folder. Running it from Downloads or a disk image can stop updates from installing.

How do I remove it?

Quit it and drag YTDownloader from Applications to the Trash. Your downloaded videos aren't affected.

Pasting links

Which links work?

Any link to a single YouTube video or a playlist: youtube.com/watch?v=..., youtu.be/..., Shorts, YouTube Music, and playlist links. Copy it from the browser's address bar or from YouTube's Share > Copy Link.

Fetch Formats spins for a long time, or nothing happens.

Looking up a video usually takes a few seconds; a long playlist can take longer. If nothing appears after a minute, check the log at the bottom for a message, click Check for yt-dlp Update at the bottom right, and try again.

Why does the Download button stay gray?

For a single video, click Fetch Formats first. Download wakes up once YTDownloader knows what the video is.

I pasted one video but it asks about a playlist.

The link includes list=, because you copied it while the video was playing as part of a playlist. The video you meant is already selected, so click Download Selected (1). Or click Back and paste the link without the &list=... part to get the full choice of qualities.

It says the video is private, unavailable or members-only.

YouTube won't give that video to anyone who isn't allowed to watch it, so YTDownloader can't either. If it's your church's own video, set it to Public or Unlisted on YouTube first.

Can I download a live stream?

Not while it's live. Once the stream ends and YouTube has finished processing it (usually within a few hours), paste the same link and download it like any other video.

It says the video is age-restricted.

Sign in when YTDownloader asks, with a Google account that is old enough to watch it. See Signing in.

Quality and formats

Which quality should I pick for ProPresenter?

1080p (Full HD). It looks sharp on projectors and TVs, plays smoothly, and keeps files a sensible size. Use 4K only for screens that are actually 4K.

Why isn't there a 4K choice?

That video wasn't uploaded in 4K. The list only shows what YouTube has for that video.

I picked 4K but got 1080p. Why?

YouTube refused to hand over the 4K stream, so YTDownloader retried with a stream that goes up to 1080p rather than fail. A blue note under the Download button says when this happened. Trying again later sometimes gets the higher quality. See While it downloads.

Why is my 60 fps video only 30 fps?

The same reason: the retry stream tops out at 1080p and 30 fps. The blue note will say so.

Why .mp4 and .m4a? Can I get .mov or .mp3?

YTDownloader always saves .mp4 (video) and .m4a (audio), because they play everywhere: ProPresenter, Keynote, QuickTime, Premiere, iPhone and Windows. If you really need an .mp3, the Music app can convert it: set Music > Settings > Files > Import Settings to MP3, select the song, and choose File > Convert > Create MP3 Version.

Can I download the video and the audio at the same time?

Yes. Hold ⌘ (Command) and click both, then click Download (2). Each is saved as its own file.

What if I don't pick a quality?

Download saves the best quality YouTube offers for that video.

Why are some files so big?

Higher qualities and frame rates make much bigger files: a 10-minute video can be around 250 MB at 1080p and about 700 MB in 4K. Pick 1080p or 720p unless you need more.

Downloading

Why is a download slow?

Mostly the length and quality of the video, and your internet speed. YouTube also sends some videos more slowly than others. A clip, or a download with captions, has an extra step at the end.

The log says "HTTP Error 403: Forbidden". Did it fail?

Probably not. YouTube sometimes refuses the usual download, often partway through. YTDownloader notices, deletes the partial files and starts again with a different stream; the log shows a line starting with >>> where it retries. If the bar ends on Done, your file is saved. If it ends with a red error and no retry line, use Help > Send Feedback.

What does the blue note under the Download button mean?

It's information, not an error. It says when a download was retried at a lower quality, or when a video had no captions to save.

Can I cancel a download?

Yes. Click Cancel. The unfinished files are deleted, so nothing half-done is left in your folder.

Can I download two things at once?

One download runs at a time. To get several videos, use a playlist, or pick several qualities of one video, and they download one after another.

Can I quit while it downloads?

Quitting stops the download. Paste the link and start it again next time.

Does it keep working if my Mac goes to sleep?

No. A download can stop or fail while the Mac sleeps. For a long playlist, keep the Mac awake and plugged in.

Playlists

Where does a whole playlist go?

Into a new folder, named after the playlist, inside your Save to folder. The files are numbered in playlist order.

Why are playlist videos only up to 1080p?

To keep big playlists quick and reliable. For a single video in 4K, download it on its own.

Some videos in the playlist didn't download.

Private, deleted or members-only videos in a playlist can't be downloaded, so they're skipped. A red note says some items may have failed; the log names them. The rest of the playlist still downloads.

Can I download only some videos from a playlist?

Yes. In the playlist list, hold ⌘ and click the ones you want (or ⇧-click to select a run), then click Download Selected.

Trimming clips

The trim preview doesn't load.

Give it a few seconds; YouTube makes new video links wait briefly before they play. If it still says it couldn't load, you can type the start and end times instead. Check for a yt-dlp update if it keeps happening.

Why does a clip take longer than I expected?

To cut exactly on the frames you chose, YTDownloader processes the clip after downloading it. A few minutes of 1080p usually takes well under a minute on a recent Mac; long clips and 4K take longer. The progress bar keeps moving while it works.

The arrow keys don't step through frames.

You're typing in a time box, where the arrows move the cursor. Click anywhere else in the Trim window and try again.

How exact is the cut?

To the frame. The clip starts and ends on exactly the frames you chose.

Can I trim an Audio only download?

Yes. Set the clip, then choose Audio only.

Can I get several clips from one video?

Yes, one at a time. Download the first clip, then click Edit Trim, set the next one and download again. Each clip's file name has its times in it, so they never overwrite each other.

How do I go back to the whole video?

Click the × beside the clip times in the main window, or Remove Trim in the Trim window.

Captions

I asked for captions, but none were saved.

That video has no English captions on YouTube, not even automatic ones. Music without words and very new uploads often don't. The video still downloads, and a note says no captions were saved for it.

How do I use the .srt file in ProPresenter or Premiere?

Keep it next to the video with the same name. Most apps import it with their own Import Captions or Subtitles command; some, like VLC, find it by themselves.

I embedded captions but don't see them.

Embedded captions are off until you turn them on in the player. In QuickTime, choose View > Subtitles. Some apps don't show embedded captions at all; use the .srt file in those.

The automatic captions have mistakes.

They're YouTube's computer-made captions, and YTDownloader saves them as they are, only fixing their timing. Open the .srt file in TextEdit to correct words before using it.

Can I get captions in another language?

Not yet. YTDownloader saves English captions only.

Files and History

Where did my download go?

Into the folder beside Save to; your Downloads folder unless you chose another. Click Reveal in Finder, or open History and click Show in Finder.

Why does Save to go back to Downloads?

Each time YTDownloader opens, it starts on your Downloads folder. Click Choose… to pick another folder for this session.

Why does the file name look slightly different from the title?

Characters a Mac can't use in file names, like : and /, are swapped for look-alikes, and the picture size is added, like [1920x1080].

I downloaded the same video twice. Did it replace the first one?

No. The second copy is saved as Name (1).mp4.

Does clearing the history delete my files?

No. Clear History only empties the list. Your files stay where they are.

Why is an entry in History gray?

That file has been deleted, moved to the Trash, or moved to another drive since it was downloaded. Files you only moved or renamed on the same drive are still found.

How far back does History go?

The last 100 downloads.

Signing in

Why is it asking me to sign in to YouTube?

YouTube sometimes checks that a real person is downloading, especially on busy or shared networks. Sign in in the window that opens, click Done at the top, and try again. See the manual.

Is it safe to type my Google password there?

Yes. That window is Google's own sign-in page, and your password goes straight to Google. YTDownloader only keeps the sign-in afterwards, the way a browser does, and only uses it to talk to YouTube.

Which account should I use?

Any Google account works. It doesn't need to own the video or be the church's account.

It keeps asking me to sign in.

Make sure you finished signing in before clicking Done. Then click Check for yt-dlp Update and try again. If it keeps happening, use Help > Send Feedback.

Updates

What's the difference between Check for Updates and Check for yt-dlp Update?

Check for Updates (in the YTDownloader menu) updates the app itself: new features and fixes. Check for yt-dlp Update (bottom right of the window) updates the engine that talks to YouTube, which needs updating more often because YouTube changes often. Both also happen by themselves once a day.

A banner says a new version is ready. What do I do?

Click Show Update to see what's new, then Install Update. YTDownloader restarts with the new version in under a minute. Or click Later to hide the banner for now. Nothing installs until you say so.

I clicked Skip This Version by mistake.

Choose YTDownloader > Check for Updates…. A check you ask for shows the update even if you skipped it.

Will updating lose my settings or history?

No. Your history, captions choices and sign-in carry over.

How do I know which version I have?

Choose YTDownloader > About YTDownloader.

This page mentions something my copy doesn't have.

The manual and FAQ always describe the newest version. Choose YTDownloader > Check for Updates… to get it. Features added recently are marked with the version that added them, like New in 1.2.

When something goes wrong

A download failed. What should I try?
  1. Read the red or blue message under the Download button.
  2. Click Check for yt-dlp Update, then try again.
  3. If a sign-in window opened, sign in, click Done, and try again.
  4. Try a lower quality, or wait a few minutes and try again.
How do I report a problem?

Choose Help > Send Feedback…. It opens an email with your app version and the recent log already included. Add what you were trying to do and the link you used, and send it.

What is the log for?

It shows the technical details of each step. You don't need to read it, but it's the first thing that helps when something goes wrong. Copy above it copies the whole log.
