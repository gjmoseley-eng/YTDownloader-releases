YTDownloader — User Manual

Video & audio downloads, no sketchy sites required

Getting Started

  1. Copy a YouTube video link. If you switch to YTDownloader right after copying it, the link will often already be pasted into the URL field for you.
  2. Click Fetch Formats.
  3. Pick a video quality (or "Audio only") from the list.
  4. Choose where the file should be saved, or leave it on the default Downloads folder.
  5. Click Download.

Download speed depends on your internet connection and the video's length and quality — larger or longer videos will naturally take more time.

Playlists

If the link you paste is part of a playlist, YTDownloader will ask what you want to do:

  • Download Entire Playlist — grabs every video, saved into its own folder named after the playlist.
  • Select specific videos — check the box next to any videos you want (⌘-click or ⇧-click to select more than one), then click Download Selected.
  • The video that was originally in your link is pre-selected for you automatically.

You can click Back at any point to return to the playlist list and change your selection before downloading.

After a Download

  • Click Reveal in Finder to jump straight to the downloaded file.
  • You'll get a notification when a download finishes — click Show in Finder right on the notification to go straight there.
  • If you download the same video and quality twice, the second copy is automatically renamed instead of overwriting the first.

Download History

Click History in the top-right corner (or press ⌘Y) to see your last 100 downloads: the title, what you downloaded, when, and which folder it went to.

  • Click Show in Finder to jump straight to the file.
  • Right-click an entry to open the file, copy the YouTube link, or remove it from the list.
  • If you move or rename a file afterward, History still finds it. If it's been deleted (or moved to the Trash or another drive), it shows in gray.
  • Clear History only clears the list. It never deletes your files.

Captions

Two checkboxes above the Save to folder add English captions to your download. The app remembers your choice.

  • Save captions (.srt) saves the captions as a separate .srt file next to the video, with the same name. This works with ProPresenter, Premiere, and most editing and presentation software, and with audio-only downloads too.
  • Embed captions in video adds the captions inside the video file. They're off until you turn them on in the player (in QuickTime, choose View → Subtitles).

If a video has captions its creator wrote, those are used. Otherwise YTDownloader uses YouTube's automatic captions and tidies up their timing. If a video has no English captions at all, it still downloads normally and a note lets you know.

Trimming a Clip

To download just part of a video, such as one song or an announcement from a long service:

  1. Fetch the video, then click Trim… above the Save to folder.
  2. A preview window opens. Play and pause with the Space bar, use the 5-second buttons to move around, and press the ← and → arrow keys to step one frame at a time (hold Shift to move one second).
  3. Click Set Start (or press I) and Set End (or press O) at the right spots. You can also type times, like 1:02:03.50 or 2:30.
  4. Click Preview Clip to watch just that part, then click Use Clip.
  5. Pick a quality and click Download.

The clip is cut exactly where you set it and saved with the times in its name, like [clip 50m00s to 52m30s], so it never replaces the full video. Cutting exactly takes a little processing, so a clip can take a bit longer than you'd expect for its length. To download the whole video again, click the × next to the clip times. Trimming works on single videos, not playlists.

Keeping Things Up to Date

  • YTDownloader checks for its own updates automatically. If one's available, you'll see a dialog with three choices:
    • Install Update — updates right away.
    • Remind Me Later — the app will ask again automatically after some time has passed.
    • Skip This Version — dismisses that specific update for good; you won't be asked about it again (you'll still be notified of future updates).
  • To see what changed in each version, choose Help → Release Notes, or click Version History in the update dialog.
  • Check for yt-dlp Update (bottom right) checks for updates to the underlying download engine, separately from the app itself. This runs automatically once a day as well.

What YTDownloader Doesn't Do

YTDownloader downloads individual videos and playlists. It's not built for downloading entire channels at once, live streams still in progress, or content that requires special access beyond a normal YouTube sign-in.

Sending Feedback

Help → Send Feedback… opens an email with your app version and recent activity log already attached, so you can just describe what happened.
